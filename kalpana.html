About the Program

Building Confident STEM Leaders

A transformative two-level program that guides female STEM undergraduates from self-discovery to career clarity, professional readiness, and national and international exposure.

Career Confidence
Innovative assignments initiate deeper self-reflection, enabling fellows to chart career paths with clarity and conviction.
Global Mentorship
STEM experts from leading global universities provide personalized guidance throughout the journey.
Peer Network
A strong community of female STEM students forming a support structure on the She for STEM platform.
100% Online & Free
World-class STEM mentoring made accessible to students from all backgrounds, at zero cost.
What is Kalpana?

Designed & delivered by scientists from leading global universities.

60+ hours of intensive learning over 8–10 weeks — making every fellow job-ready and higher-education ready. Named in honour of Kalpana Chawla, who showed that the sky is not the limit.

Our Origin Story

About
VigyanShaala

Dr. Darshana Joshi
Founder · PhD, Cambridge University

A farmer's daughter from Uttarakhand who earned her PhD in Physics from Cambridge, Dr. Joshi became the first South Asian woman elected as President of the Cambridge Graduate Students Union. Her story is the foundation of everything VigyanShaala does.

"Your background is not your barrier — it is your breakthrough."

Founded in 2015, VigyanShaala has broken barriers across India and beyond — reaching thousands of students through grants, workshops, and programs recognised by Royal Society of Chemistry, Materials Research Society USA, and the Institute of Physics UK.

2015
Royal Society of Chemistry Grants

Workshops 'A Fun Day With Science' and 'Women in Science — Leadership and Beyond' impacted 1,500 students across Patna, Agartala, Delhi, Dehradun, and Ghaziabad.

2016
Grassroots Grants — Materials Research Society, USA

'It's a MATERIALS World' workshops reached over 5,000 students across Chennai, Pondicherry, Dehradun, Pune, Mumbai and more.

2018
Vigyan Chetna Yatra & IoP UK Award

Connected with 2,000+ students in Uttarakhand. Earned Virdee grant from the Institute of Physics (UK) for teacher training in Sierra Leone.

2021
Rural STEM Champions

Launched in Berinag and Pittorgarh, Uttarakhand — connecting the remotest communities with hands-on STEM experiments and career guidance.

2024–25
Kalpana She For STEM — Christ Nagar College

89+ students from Christ Nagar College participated; 25+ promoted to the Accelerator Stage with international certification.

How It Works

Program Structure

A structured progression from enthusiast to STEM champion — 3 + 4 months of intensive, transformational learning.

01
Incubator
3 months · All enrolled students

The foundation stage — intensive blended learning, career workshops, and global masterclasses that awaken each student's STEM identity.

Open Enrollment
02
Accelerator
4 months · 15–20% selection rate

The elite stage — rigorous projects, top-1% global mentors, guaranteed internship pathways, and national / international exposure for the very best.

By Selection
03
STEM Changemaker
20–25% transformation rate

The transformation — fellows emerge as confident STEM professionals, researchers, and community leaders ready for the global stage.

What You Will Do

The Learning Journey

Highly practical, not just theoretical — every activity is designed to help students think deeply about their future and take informed decisions with confidence.

01
SWOT Analysis

Identify strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats with structured exercises that build honest self-awareness as the foundation of career decisions.

02
Career Planning

Guided activities that help students set realistic, achievable career goals — whether in academia, industry, or research.

03
LinkedIn Profile Creation

Build a compelling professional online presence. Connect with industry leaders and attract global opportunities.

04
Self Assessment

Deep-dive exercises to understand interests, values, skills, and learning style — the foundation of meaningful career choices.

05
Career Exploration

Exposure to multiple STEM paths through masterclasses, expert talks, and immersive industry interactions.

06
Masterclasses & Speaker Series

Real career journeys shared by inspiring STEM professionals — providing a grounded understanding of professional life.
